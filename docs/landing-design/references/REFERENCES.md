\# AquaFleet Landing Page — Visual References



\## Reference Priority



The images in this folder are not equal in importance.



\### 1. `01-aquafleet-target-concept.png`



This is the PRIMARY target for the AquaFleet landing/authentication page.



Use it to guide:



\- overall composition;

\- top-down research vessel placement;

\- dark ocean atmosphere;

\- marine cyan visual language;

\- left-side AquaFleet identity;

\- right-side authentication panel;

\- visual depth;

\- radar/HUD treatment;

\- cinematic quality.



The final implementation should capture the design language and composition of this image without treating it as a flattened background screenshot.



Important:

The ship, ocean, HUD, hero content, and authentication interface should remain separable implementation layers.



\---



\### 2. `02-ship-dashboard-reference.png`



Use primarily for:



\- integration of marine imagery and interface elements;

\- professional maritime atmosphere;

\- strong visual contrast between photography and UI;

\- restrained data-overlay treatment.



Do not copy its exact interface.



\---



\### 3. `03-topdown-vessel-reference.png`



Use primarily for:



\- vessel viewpoint;

\- radar geometry;

\- scan-circle concepts;

\- ocean composition;

\- scientific / operational visual language.



Avoid making AquaFleet look military.



The radar should communicate research instrumentation rather than target tracking.



\---



\### 4. `04-underwater-ui-reference.png`



Use primarily for:



\- depth;

\- atmospheric marine color;

\- subtle scientific HUD elements;

\- technical interface details.



Do not reproduce its dense HUD.



AquaFleet should remain significantly cleaner and more usable.



\---



\# Combined Design Direction



The final AquaFleet landing page should combine:



\- the composition of the AquaFleet target concept;

\- the marine authenticity of real research operations;

\- the top-down vessel presentation of the vessel reference;

\- restrained scientific instrumentation from the underwater interface;

\- a modern professional authentication experience.



The result should feel like:



> A premium marine research operations platform.



It should NOT feel like:



> A military command center, sci-fi game, generic SaaS page, or Bootstrap admin template.



\---



\# Implementation Principle



Reference images communicate visual intent.



They must not be implemented as one flattened screenshot.



Whenever practical, use independent:



\- ocean/background layer;

\- research-vessel layer;

\- wake layer;

\- radar layer;

\- HUD layer;

\- product-identity layer;

\- authentication layer.



This allows animation and responsive recomposition later.
